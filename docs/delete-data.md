# BizCard AI – Delete your data

BizCard AI (com.aistudio.bizcardai.leadtap) has no user accounts and stores all leads only on your device.

**Delete a single lead:** open the lead in the app and tap **Delete**.

**Delete all data:** uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings > Apps > BizCard AI > Storage > Clear storage.

Card images sent for AI extraction are processed in real time and not stored by BizCard AI.
